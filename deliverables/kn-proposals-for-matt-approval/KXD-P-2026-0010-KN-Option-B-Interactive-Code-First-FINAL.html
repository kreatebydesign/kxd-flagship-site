Opportunity

What this engagement is for

K&N Global Filtration is modernizing its enterprise web presence with disciplined template architecture and, in this option, a code-first reference that helps IT see real responsive behavior before production integration. This proposal (Option B — KXD-P-2026-0010) builds on the same creative scope as Option A (KXD-P-2026-0009) with an additional $10,000 investment for reference implementation, staging, and rolling repository handoffs. K&N IT owns CMS integration, production development, testing, deployment, and launch.

Situation

Where things stand

IT needs inspectable, responsive reference implementation alongside marketing-grade creative direction, without conflating reference code with production CMS work.

Engagement

How the work is organized

Option B is presented when K&N IT confirms that a Next.js/React reference repository, private staging review, and rolling source handoffs align with your approved integration workflow. K&N has not pre-approved Next.js, React, or a specific repository structure; those choices will be confirmed with IT before kickoff. Production integration remains K&N IT's responsibility.

Deliver an approved creative direction as working templates and components, with staging review and documented source handoffs, so K&N IT can implement against a known front-end reference—without assuming production CMS work by Kreate by Design.

Outcome

What you receive

IT receives organized source, demonstrated interactions, and phased releases aligned to the roadmap, without assuming unlimited revision or production deployment by Kreate by Design.

02

Delivery roadmap (November–December 2026)

Six phased milestones with responsibilities for Kreate by Design, K&N stakeholders, and K&N IT. Phases 3 and 4 run in parallel so IT can implement approved homepage and priority template families while creative production continues on remaining pages. Dates are targets contingent on timely kickoff, materials, consolidated feedback, and agreed review windows. K&N IT owns production integration, CMS/Magento work, production testing, deployment, and launch; this schedule is not a production go-live date.

Deliverables

  • Roadmap Phase 1 — Discovery and two creative directions (Nov 2–13)
    Kreate by Design: Discovery within signed scope boundaries, template-family architecture for the ~38-page program, and two distinct creative directions.
    K&N: Stakeholders, content, brand assets, and consolidated feedback through the designated project owner.
    K&N IT: Optional technical input on handoff and integration constraints.
    Approval gate: Both creative directions presented; draft template-family inventory for discovery review.
  • Roadmap Phase 2 — Direction approval (Nov 16–20)
    Kreate by Design: Refine the selected direction and prepare the direction approval package.
    K&N: Select one direction and approve in writing.
    K&N IT: Review direction pack and proposed template-family inventory.
    Approval gate: Creative direction approved; template-family inventory confirmed within agreed scope boundaries.
  • Roadmap Phase 3 — Homepage, priority templates, design system, initial handoff (Nov 20–Dec 4, parallel)
    Kreate by Design: Homepage, priority templates, responsive design system, and first developer handoff package.
    K&N: Review and approve within agreed feedback windows.
    K&N IT: Begin implementation from approved homepage and priority templates.
    Approval gate: Primary templates approved for IT implementation.
  • Roadmap Phase 4 — Remaining pages, interactions, graphics, rolling handoffs (Nov 23–Dec 18, parallel)
    Kreate by Design: Remaining template-family pages, interaction patterns, technical graphics, and rolling handoff packages.
    K&N: Rolling content support and batch approvals through the designated owner.
    K&N IT: Receive and queue approved packages as released.
    Approval gate: In-scope template families approved (Find Your Filter excluded).
  • Roadmap Phase 5 — Creative scope review (Dec 18)
    Kreate by Design: Consolidated creative scope review and punch-list preparation.
    K&N: Executive review and consolidated punch-list feedback.
    K&N IT: Alignment on integration queue and open technical items.
    Approval gate: Creative scope review accepted.
  • Roadmap Phase 6 — Final revisions and handoff (Dec 21–31)
    Kreate by Design: Agreed final revisions, documentation, and final handoff package.
    K&N: Consolidated revision feedback within the contractual review window.
    K&N IT: Receive final source materials and documentation.
    Approval gate: Creative handoff acknowledged (not production launch).

Terms

Engagement terms

Payment

Payment conditions

Investment is billed in three installments: 40% at contract execution and kickoff, 30% upon approved creative direction and delivery of agreed primary templates, and 30% upon delivery of final agreed creative assets, source materials, documentation, and completion of the contractual review process. Amounts are shown in the payment schedule and align to the total project investment.

Timeline

Project timeline

The signed agreement establishes scope boundaries before kickoff. Discovery finalizes the detailed template-family inventory and acceptance criteria within those boundaries, with written approval and change control as described in the agreement. The delivery roadmap in this proposal is the phase schedule after kickoff. Dates assume timely kickoff, K&N content and brand assets, consolidated feedback from K&N's designated project owner, and approvals within agreed review windows. If kickoff or approvals shift, dates will be re-baselined in writing. K&N IT owns production integration, testing, and deployment; this schedule does not represent a production launch date.

Validity

Proposal validity

This proposal is valid through November 6, 2026. After that date, scope, schedule, and investment may be revised.

Changes

Scope changes

Each major approval stage includes up to two consolidated revision rounds, submitted by K&N's designated project owner within the review window for that stage. Feedback must be consolidated; fragmented or conflicting direction may reset the review clock. Additions or changes outside the agreed template-family inventory, approved scope boundaries, substantial redesigns beyond agreed revisions, additional pages, new functionality, or new template families require written change approval and may adjust investment and schedule.

Intellectual property

Intellectual property

Upon full payment, K&N receives ownership of paid custom deliverables created specifically for this engagement (designs, documentation, and Option B reference source artifacts identified in the agreement), subject to third-party license terms for embedded fonts, stock, or software. Kreate by Design retains ownership of its pre-existing frameworks, reusable utilities, internal tools, and background intellectual property; K&N receives a license to use those elements only as embedded in the delivered work. Third-party components remain subject to their respective licenses. If K&N's formal SOW or procurement terms require different ownership or work-for-hire language, those terms must be reviewed and agreed in the final contract before signature.

Cancellation

Cancellation and pause terms will be defined in the final agreement, including treatment of work in progress and payments due for completed phases.
